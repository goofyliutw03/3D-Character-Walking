# Original Blender city buildings
Eight original project-authored procedural buildings, generated by `tools/assets/prepare_city.py` using the installed Blender 5.2.2 LTS. No downloaded meshes, textures, fonts or third-party source assets.

The project dedicates these generated building assets and their generator to the public domain under **CC0 1.0 Universal**: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . Blender is the authoring tool and is not the asset author.

This is a **hybrid streetscape**: only buildings come from this GLB; road, sidewalks, trees, lights and water retain the existing runtime geometry. It does not claim to reproduce an unavailable historical third-party city file. Positions use meters, Y-up, and match the existing street bounds. See asset-manifest.json for output SHA256 and generator version.
